Smooth lane-change update

- Vehicles no longer snap/teleport from their current lane into a turn lane.
- LEFT/RIGHT vehicles begin changing lane about 350 px before the intersection.
- Lane change follows an S-curve (smoothstep) while the vehicle continues moving forward.
- The vehicle rotates gradually during the lane change, then straightens before turning.
- A lane change only starts when the target lane has a safe front and rear gap.
- If the target lane is temporarily occupied, the vehicle stays in its current lane and slows gently.
- Turning starts only after the vehicle has completed the lane change.
- Ordinary lane-centering correction is gradual, not an instant coordinate clamp.
